# Saarthi Vernacular Creator Marketplace & ASCI Compliance

## 1. Overview
The Influencer & Vernacular Creator Marketplace (`PRD.md §13`, `WORKFLOW.md Flow 13`) empowers Indian MSMEs to contract regional, verified video creators (Hindi, Bhojpuri, Awadhi) across Uttar Pradesh. All brand collaborations enforce milestone escrow protection and automated statutory disclosure verification adhering to the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) Guidelines and the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

## 3. Mandatory ASCI Disclosure Verification

Under the Guidelines for Influencer Advertising in Digital Media (ASCI) and Rule 10 of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules:
- All paid promotions, barter deals, or commercial partnerships must carry clear upfront disclosure tags:
  - `#Ad`
  - `#Sponsored`
  - `#PaidPartnership`
  - `#Collaboration`
- Saarthi's `CreatorsService.verifyASCIDisclosure()` inspects live captions before approving milestone deliverables, preventing brand and creator statutory liability.
